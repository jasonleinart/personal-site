# Consulting Site Reference: Matthew Bertram Analysis

*Research Date: 2026-02-06*

Analysis of matthewbertram.com as reference for consulting.jasonleinart.com structure.

---

## What He Does Well

### 1. Single, Clear Positioning

> "Digital Information Governance™ for capital-intensive businesses"

One concept. Not a menu of services. Everything flows from this core idea.

### 2. Proprietary Frameworks with Trademarks

- Digital Information Governance™
- AI Discoverability Framework™
- LLM Visibility Stack™
- Narrative Divergence Assessment™

These signal depth and ownership. He's not offering generic "AI consulting."

### 3. Problem-First, Not Service-First

> "AI systems misinterpret business models, causing decisions to slow and trust to erode"

He leads with the risk, not the solution.

### 4. Clear Buyer Definition

- CEOs, CFOs, COOs, General Counsel
- Private equity-backed organizations
- High-stakes decision periods

Not "businesses" — specific roles in specific situations.

### 5. Minimal Nav

```
About | Advisory | Decision Integrity | Free Consultation
```

Four items. Not trying to do everything.

## Key Takeaways

1. **One problem, one framework, one engagement model** — Everything reinforces the same thesis
2. **Proprietary language** — Trademarked or branded concepts signal depth
3. **Buyer-specific** — Not "businesses" but specific roles in specific situations
4. **Problem-first** — Lead with the risk, not the solution
5. **Minimal nav** — Don't try to do everything on one site
